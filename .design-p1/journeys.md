# Journeys (Rouh's draft, from Petar's request; most frequent first)

1. **Who is here and what is each Rouh doing?** (most frequent) Starts: opening a live space. Question: "Who is in, and what is Maria's Rouh doing right now?" Data: avatars of people and Rouhs, each Rouh's marker on its part and its current step. Target: visible at once, 0 clicks, 3 s, no scrolling.
2. **Ask my Rouh to work on the page, and watch it.** (most frequent) Starts: in the space. Question: "Can my Rouh add the steps while I watch?" Action: type a request, press Ask your Rouh; see its intent, then its steps and edits at a person's speed; Skip to the end or Stop. Target: 1 field + 1 click; first edit visible within 5 s.
3. **Agree a meeting inside the space.** Starts: in the space. Question: "When can we meet?" Action: Find a meeting time, then Yes, this time on one of three options; see it agreed and booked. Target: 2 clicks each person, under a minute.
4. **Take back a Rouh's work.** Starts: in the space, after a Rouh wrote something wrong. Action: Undo its edits (that Rouh only), or History, slide back, Bring back this version. Target: 1 click for undo; 3 actions for history.
5. **Comment on a part.** Starts: on a step or the notes. Action: Comment, type, Post; the comment shows on that part for everyone. Target: 2 clicks.
6. **Connect to Maria's Rouh.** Starts: Connections. Action: Make a connect link (or show its QR code), send it; or paste a link someone sent and Connect. Target: 2 clicks to a link; 2 actions to connect.
7. **Lower or cut a connection.** Starts: Connections. Question: "How far do I trust this Rouh?" Action: change the level, or Cut, in one press. Target: 1 click.
8. **Read what was said and done.** Starts: Connections or the space. Action: open the log, in plain words, with both sides. Target: 1 click.
