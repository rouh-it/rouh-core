## What this is
Two screens of Rouh: **Connections** (which other Rouhs mine is connected to, how far each is trusted, connect links and requests, the live spaces) and **a live shared space** (a task page that two people and their two Rouhs work on at the same time, with a meeting agreed inside it).

## Who comes here, and how often
Petar and Maria (and later colleagues), on a desk and on a phone. The live space is open while working together, minutes to an hour at a time, several times a week. Connections is visited rarely: to connect, to change a level, to cut.

## The journeys (see journeys.md)

## Freedom
Rethink freely. There is no page today; nothing to keep the shape of.

## Must keep
- Presence: an avatar for each person and each Rouh in the space, now.
- Each Rouh's marker on the part it is working on; its steps shown live.
- Claims: a part claimed by a Rouh, first to reach the room wins; a person can take it back.
- Intent before a big change, with Stop.
- Comments in place, on a part.
- History slider, and Bring back this version.
- Undo one Rouh's edits, not the people's.
- Pacing: "Skip to the end" while a Rouh writes.
- Meeting: three options, each person taps yes on their own screen; agreed when all said yes to the same time; each Rouh books in its own person's calendar.
- Connections: connect by link, QR code, or a contact's Rouh address; trust level Stranger, Known, Colleague, Family, Delegate; lower or cut in one press; block.
- The signed log, in plain words, both sides readable.
- Strangers and the open network shown as off, with why.

## Style
Rouh's own: dark by default with a light theme. Turquoise is Rouh's own colour (the orb); a partner's Rouh gets a second colour (amber), the same one the orb takes while it works. People are round avatars, Rouhs are rounded squares, so they differ by shape, not colour alone. System fonts only, no network requests. Desk first; works at 390 px.

## Real counts
Phase 1: 2 people, 2 Rouhs per space; a task page with up to ~30 steps and notes of a few hundred words; up to 5 members later (phase 2). A connection list of 1 to 20 Rouhs.
