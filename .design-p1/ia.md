# Structure

## /link/space/<id>: the live space (one page)
- Top: title, the live line (a sentence: who is here, what each Rouh is doing), presence avatars (people round, Rouhs square, partner's Rouh amber); press an avatar to follow it.
- Main column, the work: Steps (each with its marker, claim, comment), Notes (live text), Meeting (proposal with three options and each person's yes).
- Side column (below the work on a phone): Your Rouh (ask box; while working: its intent with Stop, its live steps, Skip to the end), People and Rouhs here (role, Undo its edits), Comments, History (slider and Bring back), Invite (holder only), the log (drawer).
- States: invited (Join banner), the room is off (banner: your changes wait and go when it is back), ended.

## /link: Connections (one page)
- Headline sentence: how many connected, how many ask, your Rouh's name and address.
- Needs you: requests to connect (Yes / No), invitations into spaces (Open).
- Connect: Make a connect link (with QR code and copy), Bring a link (paste, Connect), Connect from a contact (Rouh address, Connect).
- Your connections: each with level (select), side, Cut, Block, the log (drawer with both sides).
- Live spaces: list, Open, and New live task page.
- Off for now: strangers and the open network, with the reason.

## Must keep -> where
Presence, markers, live steps -> space top and Your Rouh; claims -> on each step; intent + Stop -> Your Rouh and a banner at the top; comments -> on each part; history -> side column; undo per Rouh -> People and Rouhs here; pacing skip -> Your Rouh; meeting -> main column; link/QR/contact -> Connections, Connect; trust lower/cut -> each connection; log -> each connection and the space; strangers off -> Connections, Off for now.
