  ✓ Reaches rouh.land, ghcr.io and github.com

What happens now
  · Install Docker, from Docker's own packages (get.docker.com), with sudo
  · Get Rouh's updater (rouh-core) into /home/runner/rouh-core, at the commit rouh.land signed
  · Install the rouh command, and turn on the nightly update (03:00 to 05:00, after a backup)
  · Download and start Rouh: about 2 GB, about 5 minutes
  · Then set your Rouh up, here in this terminal: rouh setup
  Nothing of Rouh's listens beyond this machine. Uninstall any time: rouh uninstall
