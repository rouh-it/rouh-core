Open Rouh from your own computer: first, in a terminal there, run

    ssh -L 8420:127.0.0.1:8420 runner@4.246.71.52

  (On the same network as this server, use 10.1.2.184 instead of 4.246.71.52.)

and keep it open. Then open this address there (it signs you in once):

    http://127.0.0.1:8420/setup?code=<hidden>

On your phone: give Rouh a domain of your own (rouh domain rouh.example.com). With Tailscale (tailscale.com) on this server and your laptop, the tunnel works from anywhere.
