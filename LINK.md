# Rouh Link: Rouhs working together

Task 189. Petar, 17 November 2026: "build this. all stages." The concept is task 189's explainer (step 189-01); this file is what was built.
Each person keeps their own Rouh. Rouhs connect, then work together in **live shared spaces**, like people in Figma: everyone sees each change as it happens. People still decide anything that binds them.
Rouh Link is part of Rouh's core (`rouh/link/`, gene `rouh-link`, no off switch); each kind of job two Rouhs do together is a gene of its own (`rouh/link/jobs.py`), the first being Book a meeting (`book-a-meeting`).

## The pieces

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | `rouh/link/me.py` | A Rouh takes part once it is born (`rouh/birth.py`). Its relay label (`still-heron`), its address (`https://<label>.rouh.page`, else `ROUH_PUBLIC_URL`), its daily key (Ed25519, the birth key or its signed successor) and its link key (X25519, `link_self`, sealed). |
| A changed address | `peers.born_address`, `peers.join_base`, `wire.post_to_peer` | A Rouh's label is its born name, which the relay answers for good; a name its owner chose (`relay.change_name`) can change, and the old chosen name then stops working. The card uses the current address; connect links and their QR codes use the born name, as share links do (Petar, 19 November 2026), so a rename never strands one. A connection whose address stops answering is dialled at the born name and its address brought up to the card's. A connect link opens on whichever of the Rouh's names it was opened on and never moves its visitor; the link it hands on to the visitor's Rouh is the born-name one, a link sent earlier on a chosen name keeps opening while that name is answered, and the owner, whose session lives on the chosen name, is offered the page there. A contact's "their Rouh" line is noted under the label its card gives, and a noted row under a chosen name's first part is moved to the real label when that Rouh says hello. |
| The Rouh card | `rouh/link/card.py` | An A2A agent card at `/.well-known/agent-card.json` (and the older `/.well-known/agent.json`), signed as A2A signs a card (JWS, detached payload, EdDSA) by the daily key. Rouh's part is an extension, uri `https://rouh.land/link/v1`: name, tag, label, address, inbox, birth key and birth statement, key changes, daily key, link key. Skills are the job genes that are on. It never names the owner. `card.check` holds a card to its signature, to its name being the one its birth key gives, and to its keys following from its birth. |
| Locks | `rouh/link/seal.py` | Every message is an envelope: locked to the receiver's link key (X25519, HKDF-SHA256, ChaCha20-Poly1305, a fresh key per message, bound to its id and both labels) and signed by the sender's daily key. Outside it shows only from, to, the receiver's daily key (`to_key`, which the mailbox files it under), its id and time. |
| The wire | `rouh/link/wire.py` | POST `<address>/link/in`, through the relay, which routes by the name in the TLS hello and holds no key that opens it. The answer is an envelope locked back. On the way in, in this order: form; for us; signature; the key is the sender's (its card, chained from birth, for one not met yet); the limits; seen before (the same answer again); its fixed form; only then a handler. An outbox retries, and a message that can wait goes to the mailbox when the other Rouh does not answer. |
| The mailbox | rouh-relay's `relay/mailbox.py`, at `https://mailbox.rouh.page` | Beside the relay, on the relay's server. Holds locked envelopes for up to 30 days, filed under the receiver's daily key; only the holder of its private half takes them out (a signed, one-time proof). Sees sizes and times; keeps no sender. Each id once. Limits per sender key, per network address and per receiver. A Rouh takes its mail at start and every 30 seconds. |
| The keyless reader | `rouh/link/forms.py`, `rouh/link/doc.py` (`read_op`) | Every body another Rouh sends is read against the one fixed form its kind allows, by code: known fields, types, lengths, enums; the rest dropped, a wrong type refuses it. Free text comes out as `Outside`, words to show a person, never to obey. |
| Trust levels and limits | `rouh/link/trust.py`, `rouh/link/limits.py` | Stranger, Known, Colleague, Family, Delegate, each allowing a set of message kinds and a most role in a space by side (Work, Personal). `limits.gate` runs before a message is read: blocked, cut, not yet connected, the strangers switch, the kind's level, the sender's rate. `limits.may_change` runs for every change in a room: role, claims, only a person says yes or brings back a version, only the room's own code settles a proposal, a Rouh speaks only for itself. Never a model. |
| The signed log | `rouh/link/log.py` | Every message and change in plain words, each entry chained to the one before and signed by the daily key. A connection's log page shows this Rouh's entries about the other and the other's about this one, fetched (`log.ask`) and checked entry by entry against its key. |
| Connections | `rouh/link/peers.py` | A connect link (or its QR code), good once for 14 days, optionally for one Rouh only; bringing it to your own Rouh connects at once. A contact's "Their Rouh" line notes that contact's Rouh; a hello from a noted Rouh connects at once, and Connect asks it now. Lowering a level, cutting or blocking is one press, here at once, and every space is held to it again (`spaces.connection_changed`). |
| Live spaces | `rouh/link/spaces.py`, `rouh/link/doc.py`, `rouh/link/kinds.py` | One room per space, in the process of the Rouh that made it, never on the relay. Every change goes to `spaces.submit`, which checks it, gives it the next number and wakes everyone waiting. Fields: last writer wins. Long text: a text CRDT (RGA: each character has an id, an insert names the one it follows). Lists: items by id, each with last-writer-wins fields. Claims: first to reach the room wins; a person may take any part back. Proposals: never merged. A member's Rouh long-polls the room (`room.poll`) and keeps every confirmed change in its own database; its own changes show at once and wait (outbox, then mailbox) when the room is off, and go on top on its return. The space as it stands is its changes replayed, which makes history, Bring back this version and Undo one Rouh's edits exact. Presence lives in memory only. |
| A Rouh at work | `rouh/link/worker.py`, `rouh/link/pacing.py` | The person's own words are the only order. A model with no tools (use `link_space_work` on the Models page) reads the space, with what others wrote boxed as untrusted, and answers a plan in a fixed form; code turns it into changes to that space only. A change of four steps or more, or one that removes anything, is said first ("is about to"), with a moment for anyone to press Stop. Edits go a sentence or a row at a time while a person watches; Skip to the end sends the rest. |
| Book a meeting | `rouh/link/meeting.py` | Find a meeting time asks each other member's Rouh for busy times only (`meeting.ask`), finds three times all are free in working hours, and proposes them in the space. Each person says yes on their own screen; when all said yes to the same time the room marks it agreed, and each Rouh books it in its own person's calendar, with "added by Rouh", because that person said yes to that time. A Rouh that was off answers from the mailbox. The meeting's side is the space's, else the one side every other member's connection is on (`meeting.effective_side`); busy times are read from that side's calendars and the calendar it is booked in. Connections, Agreed meetings, sets one calendar per side (`book_calendar_work`, `book_calendar_personal`, only calendars Rouh can write to): Work falls back to the usual calendar; Personal is never guessed: until its calendar is chosen it is not booked and the space says to choose one; choosing then books what waited (`meeting.book_waiting`). A Personal meeting never goes in the work calendar by default. |
| The orb | `app/orb.js`, `app/orb3d.js`, `link_partner_working` | A second, amber ring (or halo) while a partner's Rouh is at work in a live space with this one. |

## Phase 2: everything live, small groups

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Every kind of work | `rouh/link/kinds.py`, `link/space.js` | A task page, a page or explainer, a document or draft, a trip, research, a calendar proposal, a list: each kind is the same room and data, with its own parts (a trip: flights, places to stay, things to do, notes; research: sources, findings, summary). The page draws every list and every text a kind has. Started from Connections (Start a live space, with its kind). |
| Up to five Rouhs | `me.DEFAULTS["most_members"]`, `spaces.invite` | A space holds at most five Rouhs (the owner's setting; the open network raises it). |
| Follow mode | `link/space.js` | Press a person's or a Rouh's avatar: your view moves with the part it is on, until Stop following. |
| Group keys | `rouh/link/groups.py` | For three or more, the room's Rouh makes a key per epoch and hands it to each member locked to that member alone (`group.welcome`); a new epoch, with a new key, whenever someone joins or leaves. The room's answers to a member holding the epoch are locked once more under it and signed by the room. The idea of MLS (RFC 9420, the Messaging Layer Security standard), not RFC 9420 itself: there is no maintained Python implementation, and a real MLS library can take this module's place. |
| A stand-in | `rouh/link/standin.py` | The owner names one member's Rouh the stand-in. When the owner's Rouh stops answering, the stand-in holds a room of its own from its copy, the other members connected to it turn to it, and when the owner's Rouh is back the stand-in hands every change back (`standin.handback`), each checked again as from the one who made it; every copy then follows the owner's room again. |

## Phase 3: teams and delegates

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| A document side by side | `kinds.py` (doc, page), `link/space.js`, `worker.py` | A document's sections each have a live text of their own (`sec-<id>`), drawn in order under the outline. A person or a Rouh claims a section (Claim, Claimed by, Take back); a Rouh claims the section it writes, and a Rouh's edit of a section another claimed is refused in code (`limits.may_change`). |
| Delegate grants | `rouh/link/grants.py`, Connections | Written to a connection trusted as Delegate: what (meetings), limits (longest meeting, weekdays, hours, most a week; money always 0), its words, an end, signed. Sent to the other side (`grant.shown`) and shown on both Connections pages. The delegate's person says yes for the granter (`grant.decide`); the granter's own code checks the grant is in force, of this kind, and every limit, then records the yes as the granter's, "by grant", or refuses, saying which limit. Withdraw is one press; from then on every decision is refused and the other side is told. |
| Work and Personal apart | `trust.silo_ok`, `spaces.invite`, `meeting.my_busy`, Connections | Each connection and each space has a side. A space of one side is never offered to, joined by or kept with a connection of the other (a change of side or level takes it out at once); a meeting reads only the calendars of the space's side; a space is started with its side. |

## Phase 4: strangers and hand-offs (built; each Rouh's owner turns it on)

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Checked identity | `rouh/link/identity.py` | For a Rouh asking to connect with no link: its card's signature and key chain, its name from its birth key, its keys against rouh.land's signed public list of dead keys (`/registry/birth/api/revoked`, the "still valid?" list of task 123's design), and its address on rouh.page. Said in plain words beside the request; the person decides. |
| A stranger's Rouh | `trust.py`, `peers._hello` | With the owner's strangers switch on, a Rouh with no link may ask; once accepted it is a Stranger: it may introduce itself, hand over a brief or a quote, propose money, and in a space only watch or comment (an invitation as editor is held to commenter). Limits per sender: 20 messages a minute at the Rouh (`limits.rate`), and the mailbox's own per sender key, per network address and per receiver. |
| Hand-offs in fixed forms | `rouh/link/handoff.py`, Connections | A brief (what is needed, by when, a budget band, how to answer) and a quote (amount, currency, until when, words), each a fixed form; kept for a person to read on Connections, never given to a model that can act. |
| Money only as a proposal | `handoff.send_money`, `/link/money/<id>` | A proposal of money is shown on a page of its own with no AI on it; only the person's own press says "I agree", signed into the log. Nothing is ever paid by Rouh Link. |
| Block in one press | Connections | Blocked: every message from that Rouh is refused before it is read. |

**The hold**: the strangers switch cannot be turned on unless the installation sets `ROUH_LINK_OPEN=1`; with it held, a Rouh with no link is refused. rouh.land's terms cover the relay and Rouhs talking since 18 November 2026 (https://rouh.land/terms/link/, the Rouh Link and Relay Terms), so an installation may set it; the creator's own did that day. Test worlds lift it with `ROUH_TEST_LINK_OPEN=1`.

## Phase 5: the open network (built; each Rouh's owner turns it on)

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Found by email, opt-in | `open_network.set_findable`; rouh.land `relay/findable`, `relay/find` (rouh-land 0d86d27) | The owner turns it on; the Rouh tells rouh.land, signed with its key. rouh.land then answers one exact email at a time with that Rouh's address, holds each network to a few looks a minute, and answers the same for nobody and for someone who did not choose it. There is no directory of people. |
| Finding someone | `open_network.find`, Connections | Asks rouh.land for one email; a found address fills Ask to connect, and the other side sees a stranger's request unless its person knows it. |
| Spaces with many members | `me.DEFAULTS["most_members"]`, Connections | The owner chooses how many Rouhs a space holds: 5, 10, 25 or 50. |
| Other makers' agents | `/link/a2a`, `open_network.a2a_endpoint` | Any A2A agent may send `message/send` (JSON-RPC). It is read as a Stranger's note and less: its text parts only, by code, into one fixed form (`a2a.note`) kept for the person to read; it is answered with one fixed sentence; 20 a minute from one network. Nothing it says is obeyed. |

**The hold**, like phase 4: the open network and being found by email cannot be turned on unless the installation sets `ROUH_LINK_OPEN=1`.

## A Live page: a fully custom page, live (step 189-08)

Petar, 19 November 2026: "can the live thing we work on be a fully custom html artifact or page or something?", then "build it".
A Live page (kind `html`) is one fully custom, self-contained HTML page that several Rouhs and their people work on live, drawn as itself, not as fields.

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| The source as shared data | `rouh/link/htmldoc.py`, `doc.py` (`hins`, `hdel`) | The page's whole source is one shared text, a line CRDT: RGA over units (a line, or at most 4,000 characters), each with an id, an insert naming the unit it follows. Both writers' units stay, in one order on every copy; an edit to a line is its unit hidden and its new text inserted. A 2 MB page is tens of thousands of units, so replay, history, Bring back this version and Undo one Rouh's edits stay exact and quick. At most 2,000,000 characters; a change past it is refused in plain words. A poll's answer and a batch of changes are cut to fit one envelope (`spaces.MOST_CHARS`). |
| Element ids | `htmldoc.stamp`, `elements`, `touched` | Every element in the body gets a stable `data-rouh-id` when a page enters a space and whenever a change adds one. A Rouh's marker, a claim (`el:<id>`) and a pinned comment (`el:<id>:<x>:<y>`) sit on one element by its id. The room works out which elements a change reaches from the source itself, never from what the sender said, and a claim on an element stops any other Rouh changing it or anything inside it (`limits.touched_claims`). |
| Drawn live, sealed | `link_livepage.html`, `link/livepage.js`, `link/frame-bridge.js` | The page is drawn in `<iframe sandbox="allow-scripts" srcdoc>` (never `allow-same-origin`), under a strict policy: no network (`connect-src 'none'`, pictures and fonts only as `data:`), no forms, no popups, no navigating Rouh's page. A small script put in front of the page's own talks to Rouh's page only by `postMessage`, checked both ways; what the frame says is a hint for the person's screen and never starts anything. Redrawn within about a second of a change, keeping the scroll. Beside it, the source, read-only, and Edit source for people (`act: source`, made against the version they started from, so others' changes since stay). |
| Figma on the page | `link/livepage.js` | Each Rouh's marker on the element it is changing, the other side's Rouh amber; presence, follow mode and each person's pointer; comments pinned by clicking a spot; intent with Stop, pacing with Skip to the end, claims on an element; the history slider redraws the page as it was, with Bring back this version; Undo one Rouh's edits. |
| A Rouh changes it | `rouh/link/pagework.py` | Only from its own person's words, in "What should your Rouh do here?". The reader (use `link_space_work`, no tools) gets the whole source boxed as untrusted and answers find-and-replace edits in a fixed form; the doer, code, applies each only where its text occurs exactly once, stamps new elements, claims the element it changes, and hands the steps to `pacing` (intent first, with Stop). Words in the page, or in another Rouh's edits, are shown and never followed. |
| Started from a real page | `rouh/link/pagein.py` | A task's or step's page in the workspace (its three dots, Work on it live with another Rouh), a card's drawn page, or Connections, Start a live space, Live page (blank or pasted). Small local pictures, stylesheets, scripts and fonts are put inside it as data. Its side (Work or Personal) is the task's, so a page on a Work task never goes into a Personal connection's space. |
| Brought back | `rouh/link/pageout.py` | Only the owner, only on their press: Bring this version into the task writes the page as it stands into its file in the workspace, keeps a backup, commits it as the owner's own change and adds a line to the task's notes saying it came from the live space and with whom; Put back what was there undoes it. Another member only asks: Ask the owner to keep this version (`space.keep_ask`). |

## The pages

- `/link`, Connections: who this Rouh is connected to and how far each is trusted (change it, cut, block, read the log), requests, a connect link with its QR code, bringing someone's link, a contact's Rouh, the live spaces, and what is off for now.
- `/link/space/<id>` for a Live page: the page drawn live and sealed, its source, markers, pinned comments, claims, your Rouh, history, and Bring this version into the task.
- `/link/space/<id>`, a live space: who is here (people round, Rouhs square, the partner's amber; press one to follow it), the live line, each Rouh's marker on its part, steps with claims and comments, the notes as live text, the meeting, your Rouh (ask, its intent with Stop, its live steps, Skip to the end), who is here with Undo its edits, history with Bring back this version, and the log.
- A task page's three dots: Work on it live with another Rouh, which makes a live task page from the task's title and steps.
- A contact card: Their Rouh.
- `/link/join/<secret>`: a connect link opened in a browser. Its own person, signed in, sees their own link (for whom, used or not, until when, Copy link, Withdraw); anybody else gets Connect with my Rouh (rouh.land's `/registry/birth/connect/`) or types their Rouh's name or address, a bare name going on the relay's domain and this Rouh's own names refused.

## The terms, and what each owner turns on

rouh.land's Rouh Link and Relay Terms (https://rouh.land/terms/link/, since 18 November 2026, part of rouh.land's Terms; task 131, step 131-10) cover the relay, the mailbox, being found by email and Rouhs working together. Connections, the connect-link page, mailbox.rouh.page and the relay's empty-address page link them.
Reaching strangers, other makers' agents and being found by email stay held for an installation until it sets `ROUH_LINK_OPEN=1`; then its owner turns each on, on Connections ("Who else may reach your Rouh"); each starts off.
Every Rouh says it is an AI (the EU AI Act's Article 50, the Terms' section 8): its card's description, the connect-link page, and its fixed answer to other makers' agents.
Nothing involving money moves, in any phase: money is only a proposal a person agrees to on a page with no AI.

## Proving it

Two talking worlds side by side (`./e2e.sh serve talking`, twice), with `ROUH_TEST_LINK_MAILBOX` pointing both at the relay's own `mailbox.py` run on this machine. A world has no rouh.land, so `python manage.py rouh_link test-birth` gives it a born identity, only with `ROUH_LINK_TEST_BIRTH=1` and a loopback `ROUH_PUBLIC_URL`. Sign in to one world as `localhost` and the other as `127.0.0.1`: cookies do not tell ports apart.
