# Rouh Link: Rouhs working together

Task 189. Petar, 17 November 2026: "build this. all stages." The concept is task 189's explainer (step 189-01); this file is what was built.
Each person keeps their own Rouh. Rouhs connect, then work together in **live shared spaces**, like people in Figma: everyone sees each change as it happens. People still decide anything that binds them.
Rouh Link is part of Rouh's core (`rouh/link/`, gene `rouh-link`, no off switch); each kind of job two Rouhs do together is a gene of its own (`rouh/link/jobs.py`), the first being Book a meeting (`book-a-meeting`).

## The pieces

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | `rouh/link/me.py` | A Rouh takes part once it is born (`rouh/birth.py`). Its relay label (`still-heron`), its address (`https://<label>.rouh.page`, else `ROUH_PUBLIC_URL`), its daily key (Ed25519, the birth key or its signed successor) and its link key (X25519, `link_self`, sealed). |
| The Rouh card | `rouh/link/card.py` | An A2A agent card at `/.well-known/agent-card.json` (and the older `/.well-known/agent.json`), signed as A2A signs a card (JWS, detached payload, EdDSA) by the daily key. Rouh's part is an extension, uri `https://rouh.land/link/v1`: name, tag, label, address, inbox, birth key and birth statement, key changes, daily key, link key. Skills are the job genes that are on. It never names the owner. `card.check` holds a card to its signature, to its name being the one its birth key gives, and to its keys following from its birth. |
| Locks | `rouh/link/seal.py` | Every message is an envelope: locked to the receiver's link key (X25519, HKDF-SHA256, ChaCha20-Poly1305, a fresh key per message, bound to its id and both labels) and signed by the sender's daily key. Outside it shows only from, to, the receiver's daily key (`to_key`, which the mailbox files it under), its id and time. |
| The wire | `rouh/link/wire.py` | POST `<address>/link/in`, through the relay, which routes by the name in the TLS hello and holds no key that opens it. The answer is an envelope locked back. On the way in, in this order: form; for us; signature; the key is the sender's (its card, chained from birth, for one not met yet); the limits; seen before (the same answer again); its fixed form; only then a handler. An outbox retries, and a message that can wait goes to the mailbox when the other Rouh does not answer. |
| The mailbox | rouh-relay's `relay/mailbox.py`, at `https://mailbox.rouh.page` | Beside the relay, on the relay's server. Holds locked envelopes for up to 30 days, filed under the receiver's daily key; only the holder of its private half takes them out (a signed, one-time proof). Sees sizes and times; keeps no sender. Each id once. Limits per sender key, per network address and per receiver. A Rouh takes its mail at start and every 30 seconds. |
| The keyless reader | `rouh/link/forms.py`, `rouh/link/doc.py` (`read_op`) | Every body another Rouh sends is read against the one fixed form its kind allows, by code: known fields, types, lengths, enums; the rest dropped, a wrong type refuses it. Free text comes out as `Outside`, words to show a person, never to obey. |
| Trust levels and limits | `rouh/link/trust.py`, `rouh/link/limits.py` | Stranger, Known, Colleague, Family, Delegate, each allowing a set of message kinds and a most role in a space by side (Work, Personal). `limits.gate` runs before a message is read: blocked, cut, not yet connected, the strangers switch, the kind's level, the sender's rate. `limits.may_change` runs for every change in a room: role, claims, only a person says yes or brings back a version, only the room's own code settles a proposal, a Rouh speaks only for itself. Never a model. |
| The signed log | `rouh/link/log.py` | Every message and change in plain words, each entry chained to the one before and signed by the daily key. A connection's log page shows this Rouh's entries about the other and the other's about this one, fetched (`log.ask`) and checked entry by entry against its key. |
| Connections | `rouh/link/peers.py` | A connect link (or its QR code), good once for 14 days, optionally for one Rouh only; bringing it to your own Rouh connects at once. A contact's "Their Rouh" line notes that contact's Rouh; a hello from a noted Rouh connects at once, and Connect asks it now. Lowering a level, cutting or blocking is one press, here at once, and every space is held to it again (`spaces.connection_changed`). |
| Live spaces | `rouh/link/spaces.py`, `rouh/link/doc.py`, `rouh/link/kinds.py` | One room per space, in the process of the Rouh that made it, never on the relay. Every change goes to `spaces.submit`, which checks it, gives it the next number and wakes everyone waiting. Fields: last writer wins. Long text: a text CRDT (RGA: each character has an id, an insert names the one it follows). Lists: items by id, each with last-writer-wins fields. Claims: first to reach the room wins; a person may take any part back. Proposals: never merged. A member's Rouh long-polls the room (`room.poll`) and keeps every confirmed change in its own database; its own changes show at once and wait (outbox, then mailbox) when the room is off, and go on top on its return. The space as it stands is its changes replayed, which makes history, Bring back this version and Undo one Rouh's edits exact. Presence lives in memory only. |
| A Rouh at work | `rouh/link/worker.py`, `rouh/link/pacing.py` | The person's own words are the only order. A model with no tools (use `link_space_work` on the Models page) reads the space, with what others wrote boxed as untrusted, and answers a plan in a fixed form; code turns it into changes to that space only. A change of four steps or more, or one that removes anything, is said first ("is about to"), with a moment for anyone to press Stop. Edits go a sentence or a row at a time while a person watches; Skip to the end sends the rest. |
| Book a meeting | `rouh/link/meeting.py` | Find a meeting time asks each other member's Rouh for busy times only (`meeting.ask`), finds three times all are free in working hours, and proposes them in the space. Each person says yes on their own screen; when all said yes to the same time the room marks it agreed, and each Rouh books it in its own person's calendar, with "added by Rouh", because that person said yes to that time. A Rouh that was off answers from the mailbox. |
| The orb | `app/orb.js`, `app/orb3d.js`, `link_partner_working` | A second, amber ring (or halo) while a partner's Rouh is at work in a live space with this one. |

## Phase 2: everything live, small groups

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Every kind of work | `rouh/link/kinds.py`, `link/space.js` | A task page, a page or explainer, a document or draft, a trip, research, a calendar proposal, a list: each kind is the same room and data, with its own parts (a trip: flights, places to stay, things to do, notes; research: sources, findings, summary). The page draws every list and every text a kind has. Started from Connections (Start a live space, with its kind). |
| Up to five Rouhs | `me.DEFAULTS["most_members"]`, `spaces.invite` | A space holds at most five Rouhs (the owner's setting; the open network raises it). |
| Follow mode | `link/space.js` | Press a person's or a Rouh's avatar: your view moves with the part it is on, until Stop following. |
| Group keys | `rouh/link/groups.py` | For three or more, the room's Rouh makes a key per epoch and hands it to each member locked to that member alone (`group.welcome`); a new epoch, with a new key, whenever someone joins or leaves. The room's answers to a member holding the epoch are locked once more under it and signed by the room. The idea of MLS (RFC 9420, the Messaging Layer Security standard), not RFC 9420 itself: there is no maintained Python implementation, and a real MLS library can take this module's place. |
| A stand-in | `rouh/link/standin.py` | The owner names one member's Rouh the stand-in. When the owner's Rouh stops answering, the stand-in holds a room of its own from its copy, the other members connected to it turn to it, and when the owner's Rouh is back the stand-in hands every change back (`standin.handback`), each checked again as from the one who made it; every copy then follows the owner's room again. |

## The pages

- `/link`, Connections: who this Rouh is connected to and how far each is trusted (change it, cut, block, read the log), requests, a connect link with its QR code, bringing someone's link, a contact's Rouh, the live spaces, and what is off for now.
- `/link/space/<id>`, a live space: who is here (people round, Rouhs square, the partner's amber; press one to follow it), the live line, each Rouh's marker on its part, steps with claims and comments, the notes as live text, the meeting, your Rouh (ask, its intent with Stop, its live steps, Skip to the end), who is here with Undo its edits, history with Bring back this version, and the log.
- A task page's three dots: Work on it live with another Rouh, which makes a live task page from the task's title and steps.
- A contact card: Their Rouh.
- `/link/join/<secret>`: a connect link opened in a browser; it says what it is and sends the person to their own Rouh.

## Off for now

Reaching strangers, other makers' agents and being found by email are held off for every Rouh until rouh.land's terms cover the relay and Rouhs talking (the gap task 131 found in step 131-10). `ROUH_LINK_OPEN=1` lifts the hold for an installation; then its owner turns each on, on Connections. Nothing involving money moves, in any phase.

## Proving it

Two talking worlds side by side (`./e2e.sh serve talking`, twice), with `ROUH_TEST_LINK_MAILBOX` pointing both at the relay's own `mailbox.py` run on this machine. A world has no rouh.land, so `python manage.py rouh_link test-birth` gives it a born identity, only with `ROUH_LINK_TEST_BIRTH=1` and a loopback `ROUH_PUBLIC_URL`. Sign in to one world as `localhost` and the other as `127.0.0.1`: cookies do not tell ports apart.
